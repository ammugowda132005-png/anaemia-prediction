What is Anemia?

Anemia is a condition in which you lack enough healthy red blood cells to carry adequate oxygen to your body's tissues. There are several types of anemia, including iron deficiency anemia, vitamin deficiency anemia, aplastic anemia, and sickle cell anemia, each with different causes and treatments.

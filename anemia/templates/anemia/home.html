Symptoms of Anemia

  • Fatigue: Feeling unusually tired and lacking energy.
  • Weakness: A general sense of physical weakness and lack of strength.
  • Pale or yellowish skin: Skin appears paler than usual or has a yellowish tint.
  • Irregular heartbeats: Experiencing abnormal heart rhythms or palpitations.
  • Shortness of breath: Difficulty breathing, especially during physical activity.
  • Dizziness or lightheadedness: Feeling dizzy or as if you might faint.
  • Chest pain: Experiencing pain or discomfort in the chest area.
  • Cold hands and feet: Feeling unusually cold in the extremities, especially hands and feet.
  • Headaches: Frequent or severe headaches.
